# Branded Report Generator

**Stage:** 6 · The Conversation
**Source:** Eric Story §2.1(b) — primary
**Version:** v0.1 SCAFFOLD — assembly mechanics functional; brand assets and voice standards pending collection/extraction.

## What it does

Solves the audit pain that *the deliverable is a conversation — clients see "a tax return," not the advice.* Takes any internal analysis and produces the polished artifact: CN-branded document, deck, or dashboard, in firm voice, at client reading level.

## Inputs

- Source analysis from any skill (memo, workbook, brief)
- CN brand kit `[COLLECT: logo files, color palette, fonts, letterhead, report/deck templates — request from Eric/marketing; this is collection, not extraction]`
- Voice standard `[EXTRACT: 2–3 exemplar deliverables partners consider the firm at its best]`
- Audience: owner-facing vs. board vs. lender vs. attorney (changes register and detail)

## Process

1. Select format by content and audience: letter-report (docx/pdf), deck (pptx), or dashboard (single-file HTML).
2. Restructure for the reader: findings first, support behind — clients read conclusions, partners read appendices.
3. Apply CN brand and voice; translate internal shorthand into client language; strip internal-only notes (the skill checks for them explicitly).
4. Numbers pass-through rule: every figure must match its source analysis exactly — reformatting never restates a number.
5. Output flagged DRAFT until a partner approves.

## Output

The client-ready artifact + a one-line delivery note draft, filed to war room /04-deliverables as sent.

## Guardrails

- **No new analysis at the formatting step.** If a gap appears while drafting, it routes back to the source skill — the report generator never fills it.
- Internal flags, extraction markers, and working notes are stripped and checked twice; nothing internal ships.
- Advisory disclaimers per deliverable type `[EXTRACT: CN's standard disclaimer language from existing deliverables]`.
