# Cross-Service-Line Integrator

**Stage:** 7 · Sticky Relationship
**Source:** Eric Story §2.1(b) — primary (he holds the A&A and CAS view personally)
**Version:** v0.1 SCAFFOLD — integration mechanics functional; handoff protocol pending extraction.

## What it does

Fixes the audit finding that goals and context should hand off cleanly across tax, wealth, and A&A — and today don't. Reads the whole war room and produces the integrated picture: what each service line knows, what each should know, and the specific handoff that should happen next.

## Inputs

- The full war room (profile, analyses, meeting history across all service lines)
- Service line rosters: who at CN owns this client's tax, A&A, CAS, advisory, wealth relationships
- Handoff trigger catalog `[EXTRACT: Eric's list of events that should trigger a cross-line conversation — sale process started, entity restructure, liquidity event coming, estate signals, audit findings with advisory implications]`

## Process

1. Build the integrated client picture: one page, all service lines — engagements active, findings that cross boundaries, the owner's goal as each line understands it (divergence here is itself a finding).
2. Scan for unconnected dots: advisory posture the tax team doesn't know · tax strategy the deal model doesn't reflect · A&A findings advisory hasn't read `[EXTRACT: the recurring miss patterns Eric has seen]`.
3. Recommend handoffs: what to hand off, to whom, with what context package — drafted as a short brief the receiving partner can read in five minutes.
4. Log completed handoffs on the Client Profile timeline so the relationship history shows them.

## Output

The integrated one-pager + a handoff queue (what · to whom · why now · context brief), reviewed by the relationship owner before anything moves.

## Guardrails

- Handoffs are proposed, not executed — the relationship owner controls introductions to their client.
- Wealth-management referrals respect the regulatory boundary `[EXTRACT: how CN handles the advisory/wealth line today]`.
- The integrated picture states conflicts honestly (tax strategy that hurts sale value, and vice versa) — surfacing tension is the job.
